Chicken Alfredo

Chicken Alfredo is a creamy and delicious dish made with tender chicken, fettuccine noodles, and a rich Alfredo sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound fettuccine noodles
  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Cook the fettuccine noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  3. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the chicken to the skillet and cook until golden brown and cooked through.
  5. Pour in the heavy cream and bring to a simmer.
  6. Add the Parmesan cheese and stir until melted and smooth.
  7. Toss the cooked fettuccine with the Alfredo sauce.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy!
