Spaghetti

Spaghetti is a classic Italian pasta dish made with long, thin noodles and a variety of sauces.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound spaghetti noodles
  • 1 jar (24 ounces) marinara sauce
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Cook the spaghetti noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef, onion, and garlic over medium heat until the meat is browned. Drain excess fat.
  3. Add the marinara sauce to the meat mixture and simmer for 10 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Toss the cooked spaghetti with the meat sauce.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy!
